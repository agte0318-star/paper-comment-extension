# Copyright Policy Draft

This project is designed as a comment layer around academic papers, not as a paper hosting or redistribution service.

## Product Rules

- Do not upload or store PDF files.
- Do not copy or store full article text.
- Do not store paywalled figures, tables, or screenshots.
- Store only paper identifiers, metadata, and user-created comments.
- v0.1 does not store quoted article text.

## Future Quote Rules

If short quote support is added later:

- Quotes must be short and tied to user commentary.
- Quotes should have strict length limits.
- Users must add original analysis or criticism.
- Rights holder takedown requests must be supported.

## Moderation Principles

- Remove copyright-infringing material.
- Remove harassment and personal attacks.
- Use keyword filtering for obvious abusive, spam, and academic fraud content.
- Require comments to address paper content, methods, data, or claims.
- Keep author responses clearly labeled when identity verification exists.
